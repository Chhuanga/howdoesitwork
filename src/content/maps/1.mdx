Each **junction** becomes a dot called a node. Each road between two junctions becomes a line called an edge, labelled with the minutes it takes to drive it.

The whole city is just dots and lines: a graph. The streets' shapes, the buildings and the parks are all gone. Only the connections and the numbers are left.
